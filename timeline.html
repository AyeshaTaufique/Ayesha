Timeline & Fun Facts

Ada's Timeline

Below is a detailed timeline of Ada Lovelace's life, from her birth in London to her early death at the age of 36.

Milestones in Ada Lovelace's Life
Year Age What Happened
1815 0 Ada was born in London to Lord Byron and Anne Isabella Milbanke.
1833 17 She met Charles Babbage and saw a demonstration of his Difference Engine.
1835 19 She married William King, who was later made the Earl of Lovelace.
1843 27 She published her famous translated notes, including the first algorithm.
1852 36 Ada passed away in London, leaving behind a legacy as a computing pioneer.

Fun Facts

  • Charles Babbage called her the "Enchantress of Numbers."
  • She predicted that computers could one day create music and art.
  • There is a computer language named "Ada" named after her.
  • She was a skilled horsewoman and loved to ride.
  • She suffered from severe migraines throughout her life.

Important Vocabulary

Algorithm
A step-by-step set of rules to solve a problem.
Analytical Engine
An early mechanical computer design by Charles Babbage.
Difference Engine
An automatic mechanical calculator designed to tabulate polynomial functions.

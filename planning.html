Project Planning & Analysis Phase

Before writing a single line of code, I completed a comprehensive planning and analysis phase for this final portfolio website. Here is the documentation of my design process.

1. Site Map & Structure

I planned a 3-page structure to ensure all coursework and reflections were thoroughly covered:

  • index.html (Home): Welcome message, favorites/least favorites, 3 new things learned, and contact form.
  • coursework.html: Deep dives into Modules 1-3, including the interactive image map and tables.
  • planning.html: This page, detailing the project analysis.

2. Target Audience & Goals

The primary audience for this website is my professor and classmates. The goal is to present my coursework clearly and demonstrate my proficiency in HTML and CSS.

3. ADA Compliance & Design Choices

To ensure the site is accessible to everyone (ADA compliance), I implemented the following:

  1. Alt Text: Every image includes descriptive alt text.
  2. Color Contrast: I used a dark navy (#1b2a49) and teal (#008080) against a light off-white background (#f4f9f9) to ensure readability.
  3. Font Choices: I used 'Inter' for body text as it is a highly readable sans-serif font, and 'Playfair Display' for headings to give it a classic, academic feel.

4. Project Wireframe / Layout Plan

Header: Large H1 title.

Navigation: Fixed dark bar at the top with links.

Main Content: Divided into H2 sections with lists and tables.

Footer: Contact information and copyright.
